Positivity in AG

Linear Series, Numerical Cones and asymptotic Invariants

Published

October 26, 2026

Introduction

Positivity is a central organizing principle in modern algebraic geometry, bridging algebraic topology, complex differential geometry (metric curvature), and birational geometry (the Minimal Model Program). This notebook follows the foundational text Positivity in Algebraic Geometry I by Robert Lazarsfeld, systematically developing the theory of ample, nef, and big divisors, convex cones in the Néron-Severi space, and asymptotic invariants of section rings.

The development progresses logically from classical intersection theory and asymptotic Riemann-Roch to convex cone geometry, asymptotic invariants, and birational decomposition theorems:

The following table summarizes the key properties, intersection-theoretic criteria, and asymptotic behaviors across the hierarchy of positivity:

Notion Geometric Characterization Numerical / Intersection Criterion Asymptotic Growth \(h^0(mD)\) Associated Cone
Ample \(mD\) defines a closed immersion into \(\mathbb{P}^N\) \(\forall V, \; (D^{\dim V} \cdot V) > 0\) (Nakai-Moishezon) \(\frac{(D^n)}{n!} m^n + O(m^{n-1})\) \(\operatorname{Amp}(X) = \operatorname{Nef}(X)^\circ\)
Semiample \(mD\) is base-point-free for some \(m \ge 1\) Pullback of an ample divisor under a contraction \(m^{\kappa}\) where \(\kappa = \kappa(X, D)\); \(R(X, D)\) is f.g. \(\operatorname{SA}(X) \subseteq \operatorname{Nef}(X)\)
Nef Degree on every curve is non-negative \(\forall C, \; (D \cdot C) \ge 0 \iff (D^k \cdot V) \ge 0\) Controlled cohomology: \(h^i(mD) = O(m^{n-i})\) \(\operatorname{Nef}(X) = \overline{\operatorname{Amp}(X)}\)
Big Iitaka dimension is maximal: \(\kappa(X, D) = \dim X\) Kodaira decomposition: \(mD \sim A + N\) (\(A\) ample, \(N \ge 0\)) \(\Theta(m^n)\); volume \(\operatorname{vol}(D) > 0\) \(\operatorname{Big}(X) = \operatorname{int}(\overline{\operatorname{Eff}}(X))\)
Pseudoeffective Lies in the closure of the effective cone Dually paired with the movable/nef cones Non-zero sections exist after arbitrary small ample twist \(\operatorname{Pseff}(X) = \overline{\operatorname{Eff}}(X)\)

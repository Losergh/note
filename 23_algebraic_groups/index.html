Introduction

Positivity is a central organizing principle in modern algebraic geometry, bridging algebraic topology, complex differential geometry (metric curvature), and birational geometry (the Minimal Model Program, MMP). This notebook follows the foundational text Positivity in Algebraic Geometry I by Robert Lazarsfeld, systematically developing the theory of ample, nef, and big divisors, convex cones in the Néron-Severi space, and asymptotic invariants of section rings.
